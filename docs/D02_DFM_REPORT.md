# D02 DFM Report

## Candidate processes

- Laser/fibre cut sheet and plate, common 90° press-brake bends, fixture TIG welding, manual finishing.
- C-frame critical interfaces are finish-drilled/reamed or machined after welding.
- Four 7 × 14 mm M6 slots provide ±3.5 mm primary alignment; two non-collinear Ø6 dowels are created after alignment.

## Structural screening

This is conservative hand-calculation screening, not certification FEA. The 10 N forming load is carried by the 4 mm C-frame rather than skins. Under a representative 100 N local service load, the 1.5 mm deck with downward returns and dry-side Ø72 reinforcement ring is expected to remain well below the available 4 mm boss/aperture clearance; a physical dial-indicator load test is required before release. Likely weak regions are door hinge/latch attachments, large rear-cover spans and welded deck distortion. Local pads/returns are included.

## Warnings/questions

- Generic K-factor flat patterns are preliminary.
- Confirm bend radii and cutout-to-bend rules for each thickness.
- Confirm access for continuous sanitary-wall/deck TIG seam and distortion control.
- Confirm C-frame fixture welding and post-weld finish machining capacity.
- HMI, glands, fans, shaft seals and A03/A04 motor-specific holes remain replaceable interfaces.
- Door hinge requires prototype sag/opening test at approximately 110°.
- D02C06 zero distance is intentional dry-side mounting-face contact at Y365, not an unclassified collision; final purchased-motor bolting remains on A01-016.
