# Design Decisions

## LOCKED

- 430 × 380 × 625 mm body; G0 and axis convention.
- AX_R X215/Y115; AX_D X215/Y205; sanitary wall Y250.
- Z178 nominal ball centre, 3.00 mm orbit, 55 mm A08 travel.
- Transfer chute outlet Y155/Z208 and free-flight region to the rounder.

## PROVISIONAL

- Simplified folded-shell thickness geometry and C-frame members.
- Load-cell platform, carriages, guides, bellows, output chute, and actuator envelopes.
- A03 retractable axial dog coupling with 30 mm service retraction.
- +175 mm Z then -100 mm Y hopper service trajectory.
- Ø38 moving boss, Ø52 deck aperture, Ø42 inner and Ø72 outer bellows seats.
- HTD 3M-class 9 mm belt, 20T/24T pulleys, adjustable A07 motor mount.
- A04 Ø68 paddle sweep within an Ø80 housing bore.
- D01 wiring-route and service-loop keep-out volumes.
- D02 R2.0/K0.42 bend compensation, preliminary flat patterns, ventilation openings and purchased-interface cutouts.
- D02 folded-pan/spine/panel returns pending press-brake and weld-distortion feedback.

## ASSUMED

- Solids represent conservative spatial envelopes where detailed interfaces are not specified.
- The D01 skeleton uses nominal coordinates without production tolerances.

## PURCHASE-DEPENDENT

- Motors, gearboxes, bearings, HMI, PSU, drivers, contactor, and control hardware.
- Final agitator dog design, bellows supplier, A07 BLDC frame and belt tensioning hardware.

## TEST-DEPENDENT

- Feeder paddles, gate edges, cartridge curvature, spring compression, and finished-product handling surfaces.
- Bellows fatigue/sanitary certification and ejector concave contact-face effectiveness.
- D02 deck deflection, C-frame dynamic response, door sag and weld distortion require prototype measurement.

## D02.1 STUDY-ONLY

- D02 remains the authoritative A01 baseline; LW1/LW2/LW3 are isolated and cannot enter the machine build without explicit approval.
- LW2 retains SS304 wet barriers and precision C-frame while limiting aluminium to dry, isolated components.
- Galvanic isolation, base torsion, 300 N deck loading and 100–320 rpm vibration run-up are approval prerequisites.
