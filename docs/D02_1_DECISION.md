## Approval decision

**LW2 was approved by the user and is now the active A01 configuration.** Its calculated study mass is 12.210 kg while retaining SS304 for every wet/food barrier and the precision C-frame. It is less structurally aggressive than LW3 and makes the aluminium boundary explicit and inspectable.

The former 20.892 kg D02 configuration remains preserved as the reference baseline builder and export. LW2 is not released for cutting: galvanic-isolation details, a 300 N deck test, base-torsion fixture test, C-frame displacement measurement and instrumented 100–320 rpm vibration run-up remain required before manufacturing release.
