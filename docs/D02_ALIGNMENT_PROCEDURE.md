# D02 C-frame Alignment Procedure

1. Place chassis on a stable level surface and level with four M8 feet.
2. Establish Datum A at the structural base, Datum B at X215, and physical front Datum C.
3. Install C-frame loosely on four M6 bolts through 7 × 14 mm slots.
4. Adjust the common frame to AX_R X215/Y115; available primary adjustment is ±3.5 mm.
5. Verify lower/upper interface coincidence and perpendicularity.
6. Snug then torque mounting bolts in a crossed sequence.
7. Drill/ream two non-collinear Ø6 dowel locations in assembled condition.
8. Install stainless dowels, recheck axis target ±0.15 mm, and record measured values.

CF_DATUM_1 is the base-pad plane, CF_DATUM_2 the left finished interface, and CF_DATUM_3 the non-collinear dowel reference. A07 and A08 inherit alignment only from these C-frame references.
