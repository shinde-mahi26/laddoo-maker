# D02 First-pass Structural Screening

This screening uses simple beam/plate idealisations, not certification FEA.

- Forming load: 10 N nominal, assessed conservatively through the common 4 mm C-frame. Expected elastic displacement is below 0.1 mm; welded-interface and alignment accuracy dominate.
- Deck service load: 100 N representative, factored to 300 N. A simplified 72 mm reinforced strip calculation gives approximately 0.6 mm upper-bound local deflection. This is below the 4 mm boss/aperture clearance but requires a dial-indicator load test on the prototype.
- Rounder excitation/belt reaction: load path remains inside C-frame/base pads. Inspect slot fretting and dowel seating during prototype endurance testing.
- Hopper: approximately 4 kg mixture load is supported through the chassis/spine concept; final hopper clamps/supports belong to D03 and require load testing.
- Door: formed perimeter frame and local hinge/latch support target less than 1 mm visible sag. Verify at ~110° opening with the selected hinge and polycarbonate panel.

Likely weak/distortion-sensitive regions are the deck/wall continuous seam, rear-cover spans, door hinge/latch attachments and C-frame weldment. Balanced welding, dry-side reinforcement and post-weld finish operations are specified.
