Baseline/LW1 imply two-person lifting. LW2/LW3 approach the desired mass but should still use recessed side handholds and labelled two-person lifting for full removal; one person may reposition an unloaded machine on a counter if ergonomics are validated. Casters are not recommended automatically. A trolley may be offered for installations needing frequent movement.
