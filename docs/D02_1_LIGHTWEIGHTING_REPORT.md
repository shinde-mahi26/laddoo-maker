## Candidates

- **LW1 — 17.954 kg:** all SS304; 1.5 mm ribbed base, 1.2 mm hat spine, 0.8 mm skins, 3 mm SS windowed C-frame with local 4 mm pads. It reduces 14.1% but misses the preferred 15 kg ceiling.
- **LW2 — 12.210 kg:** retains SS304 wet barrier, deck and precision C-frame; uses isolated AL5052 for dry spine, skins, rear cover, fascia and adapters. It reduces 41.6% and meets the preferred target.
- **LW3 — 11.034 kg:** aggressive 1.2 mm ribbed base, 1.0 mm spine, 2 mm flanged SS frame and thin dry aluminium skins. It reduces 47.2% but has the smallest structural margin and highest prototype risk.

LW2 aluminium/SS interfaces require polymer isolation washers, nonconductive barrier film or qualified coating, sealed joint design, no wet-zone exposure, controlled stainless fastener torque and service inspection for galvanic corrosion. Aluminium threads should use inserts/captive steel hardware rather than repeated direct threading.

No candidate generates cutting files. All require prototype load/vibration/corrosion review before replacing D02.
