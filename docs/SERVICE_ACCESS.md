## Rev-A1 hopper sequence

1. Isolate and empty the machine.
2. Release/remove the lid as required.
3. Retract the A03 axial dog 30 mm into the dry side.
4. Release the two hopper clamps.
5. Lift the hopper and captured food-side agitator 175 mm.
6. Translate the lifted assembly 100 mm toward -Y.

The lifted lid reaches Z783, below the previously reserved Z805 overhead limit. Locked process geometry has not been altered.

Hopper/agitator; feeder housing/liner and rotor; removable G1 blade; weigh cup; removable G2 blade; transfer chute; selected keyed upper/lower cartridge; ejector pad; output chute/tray. Motors, load cell and calibrated mechanisms remain installed.
